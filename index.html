About

I build machine learning systems inspired by how human intelligence actually works — flexible, continually learning, and able to acquire new skills without forgetting the old ones.

I'm currently Head of AI at Workfabric AI, where I lead research on compounding intelligence: inferring feedback implicitly from a knowledge worker's sequence of human–computer interactions, and using it to post-train models that behave as digital twins of individual enterprise workers.

Before this, I was the Founder & Lead Research Scientist at Legion Intelligence (previously Yurts AI), where I led research on domain-adaptable retrieval-augmented generation and on compressing LLM decoders for fully air-gapped deployments — using functionally invariant paths, the differential-geometry framework I developed during my PhD.

I completed my PhD at Caltech in computational neuroscience with Dr. Matt Thomson, a research masters at Carnegie Mellon with Dr. Ge Yang, and a B.Tech at IIT Madras.

My interests have been broad: accidental beginnings in biology, a niche in computational biology, converging on computational neuroscience, and most recently artificial intelligence inspired by biological systems. Along the way I've been fortunate to work with and be guided by some of the best minds in these fields.

Work Experience

Workfabric AI

Aug 2025 – Present

Head of AI

Palo Alto, California · Seed stage

  • Lead R&D on algorithms for compounding intelligence. Conventional RLHF is capped by explicit human feedback, which is scarce, expensive, and does not scale; we found a way past that ceiling by inferring feedback implicitly from each knowledge worker's sequence of human–computer interactions, and using it to train proxy verifiers far more expressive than reward models built on explicit signals alone.
  • Post-trained models to build digital twins of individual enterprise knowledge workers that continually learn from that worker's sequence of digital interaction data without catastrophic forgetting, in order to build trust and improve productivity.
  • Designed rigorous evaluations for agentic AI — a critical, often underinvested piece of building trustworthy agents — built on multi-step agentic rubrics and validated through human–agent comparison, to systematically measure and improve agentic reasoning and task completion.
  • Built an agentic harness with recursive self-improvement that efficiently surfs through data to retrieve the interactions and context most relevant to an agent's goal, modeled after how humans or higher-order entities (account twin, product twin) actually behave; the harness improves itself from its own traces, with gains measured across multiple benchmarks.
  • Led development of X-SYNTH, a synthesis pipeline performing combinatorial synthesis over sequences of digital interaction data contributed by enterprise knowledge workers; took it from R&D to production, where it now supports a daily load of 20K+ queries in a Fortune 500 deployment.

Legion Intelligence (previously Yurts AI)

Jun 2022 – Aug 2025

Founder & Lead Research Scientist

San Francisco, California · Seed to Series B

  • Compressed LLM decoder models to enable fully air-gapped deployments by traversing functionally invariant paths (FIP) — a differential-geometry framework I developed during my PhD (Nature Machine Intelligence, 2024) that moves a network through weight space along performance-preserving paths. This sparsified decoders to fit the constrained compute of disconnected, classified environments (e.g. DoD edge) with no loss in generation quality.
  • Led the design, development, and evaluation of domain-adaptable retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems: built lightweight training pipelines for small ranker and language models, pioneering fine-tuning and FIP-tuning techniques that improved retrieval precision and cross-domain generalization; architected the end-to-end evaluation framework behind them, with task-specific benchmarks grounded in enterprise data (PDFs, CRMs, ERPs) measuring retrieval fidelity, grounding accuracy, and factual consistency beyond academic datasets.
  • Developed and productionized document layout-aware models that leveraged structural cues (sections, tables, lists) for semantically coherent document chunking, improving retrieval granularity and downstream QA quality across heterogeneous document types.
  • Drove thought leadership through widely read technical blog posts translating RAG and LLM research into scalable engineering practice — on catastrophic forgetting, cost-efficient retrieval, hallucination metrics, and the trade-offs between RAG and long-context models.
  • Functioned as a key technical leader across research, system architecture, and MLOps; helped establish Yurts' foundational ML infrastructure and scaled the team from seed stage to 70+ employees while mentoring researchers and aligning research with product priorities.
